Module 02

PCB Designing

Convert your schematic into a physical Printed Circuit Board (PCB) and prepare for manufacturing.

Step 1

Convert Schematic to PCB

At the top of your screen in EasyEDA, click Design > Convert Schematic to PCB.

If you see a warning about nets, click "Check Net" or just proceed. Now, you should see your components inside a purple rectangle.

Step 2

Create a Custom Outline

The purple lines are your BoardOutLine. You should customize this! A custom shape makes your board unique.

  • Select the BoardOutLine layer in the sidebar.
  • Use the Track tool to draw your shape.
  • Keep your board size below 100x100mm.
Components placed inside a custom board outline.
Step 3

Routing your Traces

The blue ratlines show you the connections but they aren't real wires yet. You need to "route" them with copper tracks.

  • Select the TopLayer (Red) or BottomLayer (Blue).
  • Click 'W' or the Track tool to draw copper lines between pads.
  • If wires must cross each other, click 'V' to place a Via and switch to the other side of the board.
Step 4

Add Custom Art

You can add text and images to your board using the TopSilkLayer (the white ink).

Go to File > Import > Image to add your own logo or artwork to the board.

3D View of a finished custom PCB design.
Pro Tip: Click View > 3D View periodically to see how your physical board will actually look!
Step 5

Manufacturing Files

Once you pass the Design Rules Check (Design > DRC), it's time to export. You need a "Gerber" file.

  1. Click File > Generate PCB Fabrication File (Gerber).
  2. Click "No, Generate Gerber Anyway" if prompted.
  3. Download the zip file.
Step 6

JLCPCB Ordering

Go to jlcpcb.com and upload your Gerber zip file. You should see an instant quote!

Ensure settings match the defaults shown here.
  • Layers: 2
  • Materials: FR-4
  • PCBA: NO (We will hand-solder these!).

Once you have your price, take a screenshot of the quote with Global Standard Direct shipping selected.

Congratulations! You've designed your first hardware project.
